Section 4

How Registration Works

Players register in a 2-step process with bot protection and strong password requirements.

1

Account Details

Player enters their first name, last name, email address, and creates a password. The password must be at least 8 characters and include an uppercase letter, a number, and a special character. A real-time strength meter shows how strong the password is. A confirm password field with a match indicator prevents typos.

2

Player Information

Player fills in their date of birth, phone number, playing position, age group, and years of experience. For players under 18, a parent/guardian name, phone, and emergency contact are collected. Phone numbers are validated against a standard format.

3

Terms Agreement & CAPTCHA

The player must check a box agreeing to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. They then complete an hCaptcha challenge to prove they are human. The Register button stays disabled until both are done.

4

Account Created Automatically

Supabase creates the user account and a database trigger automatically creates a profiles row linked to the new account. The player details are then saved into that profile. This ensures the player always has a profile even if the connection drops mid-registration.

5

Redirected to Dashboard

After successful registration, the player is automatically signed in and redirected to their personal /dashboard page where they can view and edit their profile.

Section 5

How Login Works

Login is protected by hCaptcha and a server-side route guard on every request.

1

Enter Credentials

Player enters their email and password. A toggle button reveals or hides the password. A "Forgot password?" link is available to start a self-service reset flow.

2

Complete CAPTCHA

The player solves the hCaptcha challenge. The token generated is sent along with the login request. Supabase verifies this token on the server — it cannot be bypassed by disabling JavaScript.

3

Supabase Issues a Session

Supabase validates the credentials and issues a JWT (JSON Web Token) session cookie. This cookie is stored securely in the browser and sent automatically on every subsequent request.

4

Route Guard Checks Every Request

A server-side file called proxy.ts runs before every page loads. It reads the session cookie to confirm the user is logged in. If they are not, they are redirected to /login. If they try to access /admin without the admin role, they are redirected to /dashboard.

5

Dashboard or Admin Loads

Authenticated players see /dashboard with only their own data. Admins see an additional Admin link in the navbar and can access /admin to manage all players.
